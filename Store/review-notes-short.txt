3. WHAT IT IS — A weekly spending budget. You set what you can spend in a week and
the app answers one question: what is left of it. An expense is a description, an
amount, a date and an optional category. For people who budget everyday spending
weekly rather than monthly, especially couples sharing one pot: a budget can live
on several devices and stays in step across them. There is deliberately nowhere to
enter recurring bills; money coming in is entered as a negative expense, which the
app's own "How this works" screen explains.

4. ACCESS — No credentials needed; there are no accounts. To see a populated app,
launch it, choose "Join with an ID" and enter:

    72b1a062-bf95-48cb-bde1-bee2b528e200

It holds no personal data and is safe to change; creating a fresh budget instead
takes seconds. Week: "+" adds, tapping a row
edits, swipe-left deletes, long-press copies to next week, chevrons or a sideways
swipe change week. Month totals each week. Categories charts by category — tap a
slice for the expenses behind it. The gear icon holds the budget's settings,
"Create an invitation" (a one-time sharing link), Compact layout, How this works,
and About.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES — One, and it is the developer's own:
budget.andrewovens.com, a Node.js and PostgreSQL server they run on their own
hardware. It stores only what is typed in: budget name, weekly amount, start day,
and each expense's description, amount, date and category. Everything else is
Apple's — SwiftUI, SwiftData, Swift Charts, Foundation. There are NO third-party
dependencies: no analytics or crash SDK, no advertising, no authentication
provider, no payment processor, no AI service, no data provider. The server keeps
daily per-endpoint request counts to know it is working; they are counts, not
events, are tied to no person or device, and are described in the privacy policy.

6. REGIONS — No differences. No region gating or geographic restriction, English
only. Currency and date formats follow the device's own settings. A budget "day"
is handled in UTC so an expense falls on the same day on every device sharing a
budget, whatever time zone each is in.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL — Neither applies. This is a
note-keeping tool for money the user types in. It is not a financial institution
or acting for one: no bank connections, no payment processing, no card details, no
credit or transaction data, no financial advice. Nothing in it requires a licence.
It contains no protected third-party material; the icon, artwork and copy are the
developer's own.
